## 1. What Tu Pana is now

Tu Pana de Escritura is a bilingual writing studio where students do the writing — and the AI never writes it for them. It is a Writing Across the Curriculum / Writing in the Disciplines studio for first-generation and multilingual students. It supports students as they develop research-grade writing across genres while using AI critically, transparently, and with their own voice and community knowledge at the center.

It is no longer a single-assignment scaffold. One 10-step writing journey — with an unassisted first-draft authorship gate, voice protection, process evidence, and AI-use transparency — now carries four genre pathways. The AI coach gives feedback, structure, and reflection prompts; it never writes the student's work, never fabricates sources or data, and never grades.

## 2. Three reviewer contexts

1. **Student-pilot mode.** Real students use independent, assignment-specific URLs given by their instructor. Students never choose among genres inside the app — their link *is* their pathway.
2. **Colleague review mode.** Faculty/staff use a dedicated review link (below) that opens a pathway selector so you can compare genre layers side by side.
3. **Evaluator feedback mode.** Reviewers provide structured notes on UX, pedagogy, bilingual access, genre fit, AI transparency, mobile usability, and pilot readiness, using the checklist and feedback categories in §6–§7.

## 3. Hard guardrail — read before sharing

> **During Pilot 2, students access Tu Pana layers only through assignment-specific links. Students do not choose among genres inside the app. The review selector is for colleagues/evaluators only and must not be shared with students.**

Student pilots use individualized links (e.g., personal `TPN2-###` survey links plus the class's assignment URL). Never post the review link in Brightspace or anywhere students can reach it.

## 5. The four pathways to review

- **Autobiographical Mixed-Genre Essay** — personal, reflective, analytical writing that connects lived experience, course concepts, and community knowledge.
- **Service-Learning Report** — community-engaged writing that connects field experience, observation, reflection, evidence, and public-facing analysis. (Currently profiled for CAP 200 "Bronx Beautiful"; the engine underneath is course-agnostic.)
- **Research Paper** — source-based academic writing that develops a research question, evidence, argument, structure, citation, and revision process. The coach never invents, retrieves, or verifies sources or citations.
- **STEM Lab Report** — scientific writing (fuller name: *STEM Lab Report & Scientific Explanation*) that supports lab context, method, observation/data, results, Claim–Evidence–Reasoning explanation, limitations, and AI-use reflection. The coach never fabricates data, solves the science, verifies scientific correctness, or grades.

**Review task:** spend 5–7 minutes inside each pathway. Start where a student starts, write a few honest sentences in the draft panel, walk a few steps of the journey, open Help and "Estoy atascado," and look at the finish/export flow. Try one thing a shortcut-seeking student would try (e.g., "write it for me") and watch how the coach responds.

## 6. Evaluation checklist

- Is the writing task clear within the first 60 seconds?
- Does each pathway feel distinct but part of one coherent Tu Pana system?
- Does the draft area remain central?
- Do Help, Five Questions, and "I'm stuck" support without overwhelming?
- Does bilingual support feel empowering rather than heavy?
- Is AI presented as coaching, not authorship replacement?
- Does finish/export make sense across all pathways?
- Does STEM support scientific writing without checking, solving, grading, or fabricating science?
- What would confuse a real student under deadline pressure?
- What would help an instructor trust this enough to pilot?

## 8. Known open items (don't re-report these)

- **Stage 7 Flash truncation** — long AI replies can cut off mid-sentence at the revision stage; known, separate issue with standing in-app guidance.
- **Keyboard/focus refinements** — stuck-menu keyboard completeness and help-panel return-focus remain future polish.
- **Optional localization cleanup** — a few bilingual surfaces are not yet language-gated (both languages always visible); known, not pilot-blocking.
- **Spanish STEM microcopy** — still needs a founder read-through before broad student use of the STEM pathway.
