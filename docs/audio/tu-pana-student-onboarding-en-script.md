# Tu Pana Is Not Here to Write Your Essay. It's Here to Help You Write It.

*Student-facing onboarding script — TED style*
*Tu Pana de Escritura | Drafted: 2026-06-09*
*Audience: Hostos Community College students, LAC 118 Summer 2026*

---

You know that feeling.

You sit down to write. You have ideas — real ones. You know what happened, what it meant, why it matters. Maybe you've been thinking about it for days. And then you open a blank page, and everything just stops.

The ideas are there. The words are somewhere. But they won't come.

That is not a sign that something is wrong with you. That is one of the most common experiences in writing. And it does not mean you don't belong here.

---

Here's something nobody tells you: writing is not just putting words on a page.

Writing is thinking. It's taking everything you know — everything you've lived, studied, and figured out — and organizing it into a shape that someone else can follow. It means deciding what matters first. What your reader needs to understand before you can say the next thing. How to move from your personal experience to an argument that reaches beyond just you.

That is genuinely hard work. For everyone. Whether you've been writing in English your whole life, or you think in Spanish and write in English, or you move between languages depending on the moment — writing is a process. It is not supposed to be instant. And struggling with it is not the same as failing at it.

---

So. I want to introduce you to Tu Pana de Escritura.

Hold onto that word: *pana*. If you've heard it, you already know what it means. A pana is not just a friend. It's the friend who shows up. The one who stays when things get hard. The one who asks exactly the right question when you're stuck — not to embarrass you, but to help you find what you already know.

Tu Pana de Escritura is a bilingual AI writing coach built on that idea. Not a search engine. Not a shortcut. A guide that walks alongside you, asks questions, and helps you hear your own thinking.

---

Here is how it works.

Tu Pana moves through ten stages — ten steps that take you from your first idea all the way through drafting and revision. At each stage, it asks you questions.

Maybe you're writing about your neighborhood. Your family. Something that changed the way you see the world. Tu Pana won't write that for you. But it will ask: *What happened? Why did it stay with you? What do you want your reader to understand that they probably don't know yet?*

Those questions do something real. They unlock what's already in your head. They help you move from stuck to writing.

---

And I want you to hear this part clearly.

Tu Pana does not write instead of you. Tu Pana helps you hear your own thinking.

The essay is yours. The ideas are yours. The voice is yours. If you use Tu Pana the way it's designed — actually thinking through the questions, actually writing your own answers — what comes out will be genuinely yours. Something you can be proud of.

If you try to skip that part — if you paste in AI output and call it done — you'll miss the whole point. It won't feel like your work. Because it won't be.

Use Tu Pana the way you'd use a real study partner: honestly, actively, with your full mind.

---

One more thing: Tu Pana works in Spanish, in English, and in the movement between them.

If you think in Spanish, write in Spanish. If you start in one language and switch to another mid-thought — do that. Tu Pana is built for the way you actually think, not for a version of you that speaks only one language perfectly. Your linguistic reality is not a problem to solve. It is part of how you write.

---

This is a pilot. You are among the first students to use Tu Pana. That means your experience directly shapes how the tool gets better.

That's why I'm asking for two short surveys: one before you begin, one after you finish. The pre-survey is about five minutes and asks about your writing experience right now. The post-survey is about eight minutes and asks what worked, what didn't, and what you'd change.

Your answers are anonymous. Your grade is not affected. And your feedback genuinely matters — not just to me, but to every student who uses this tool after you.

---

Here is how I want to leave you.

You have something to say. You have experiences worth writing about. You have a mind that works — even when it doesn't feel that way at 11 PM with a deadline tomorrow.

Tu Pana is not here to do your thinking for you. It is here so that when you sit down at that blank page — full of ideas that won't come out — you don't have to be alone.

Use it. Think with it. Write your own story.

Your voice is the whole point.

---

## Short Version — 60–90 seconds

*For audio recording, Brightspace video announcement, or classroom opener.*

---

You know that moment — you sit down to write, you have the ideas, but nothing comes out.

That is not about intelligence. Writing is thinking. It's organizing everything you know into something someone else can follow. And sometimes you need someone to ask the right question.

Tu Pana de Escritura is a bilingual writing coach. And *pana* — if you know the word — is the friend who shows up, who asks the right question, who helps you find your way when you're stuck.

Tu Pana walks you through ten stages of writing. It asks questions. It helps you organize your ideas, draft your work, and revise with intention. It does not write your essay for you. It helps you write it yourself.

Your voice. Your story. Your work.

Before you begin: complete the five-minute pre-survey. After you finish: complete the post-survey. Your feedback helps make this tool better for the students who come after you.

Now — let's write.
