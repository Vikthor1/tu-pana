**Required Path A:** Mixed-genre autobiographical essay
**Contrasting Path B:** STEM lab report

## Canonical sources inspected

- `assets/js/genre-template.js`: `mixedGenreAutobiographicalEssay`, stable pedagogical sequence,
  authorship gate, allowed/blocked support, revision, Voice Polish, checklist, and reflection.

  connection, research/source questions, outline, unassisted Draft, revision, Voice Polish,

  journey/migration, positionality, and story connected to history/analysis as evidence.

- `docs/project-brief.md`, `docs/ideas/tu-pana-genre-selection-pathway.md`, and faculty pedagogy

  `bilingual-content-strategy.md` D17-5 (student text is inviolable; code-switching is never

- Pilot records: `docs/pilot/tier-4-pilot-testing-packet.html` and the colleague-review evidence

  Anonymized early comments report respectful treatment of bilingual phrasing; these are signals,
  not outcomes evidence.

## Reversible prototype decisions

The four-Move compression, inline onboarding wording, exact reviewer labels, contextual risk cue,
student-controlled Finish checks, and placement of protected phrases beside Planning notes are
prototype hypotheses. Founder and representative-student testing must determine whether they
preserve the pedagogical arc without restoring density or demanding cultural performance.
