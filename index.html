Las Cinco Preguntas · The Five Questions Úsalas con cada respuesta · Use with every response Apoyo · Support ▾
  1. ①¿Es preciso? · Is it accurate?
    ¿Las afirmaciones académicas o históricas son verificadas? ¿Necesitan una fuente real? Are factual or academic claims real and verified — or does this need a source? Señal de alerta: "bien documentado en la literatura" sin citar nada. · Red flag: "well-documented in the literature" with no citation.
  2. ②¿Es mi voz? · Is this my voice?
    ¿Todavía suena como la persona específica que lo escribió? Does this still sound like the specific person who wrote it? Señal de alerta: "impactó mi identidad cultural" — ¿quién más podría haber escrito esto? · Red flag: "shaped my cultural identity" — who else could have written this?
  3. ③¿Es específico? · Is it specific?
    ¿Hay detalles reales — ejemplos, datos, personas, palabras, lugares — o se queda abstracto? Are there real details — examples, data, people, words, places — or does it stay abstract? Si no puedes señalar un detalle concreto, la respuesta es demasiado general. · If you can't point to a concrete detail, it's too general.
  4. ④¿Profundiza mi argumento? · Does it deepen my thinking?
    ¿Profundiza la conexión entre experiencia personal y significado más amplio? Does this deepen the connection between personal experience and larger meaning? Describe vs. analiza: ¿explica el por qué, no solo el qué? · Describes vs. analyzes: does it explain the why, not just the what?
  5. ⑤¿Respeta lo que sé? · Does it respect what I know?
    ¿Generaliza, aplana, o pierde algo que tú sabes desde tu comunidad o cultura? Does this generalize, flatten, or miss something you know from your own community? Tu conocimiento comunitario es evidencia. El coach no puede conocerlo. · Your community knowledge is evidence. The coach cannot know it.

Bienvenido · Welcome

Your coach is powerful.
Your judgment is more powerful.

Tu Pana te ayuda a pensar y revisar — pero no ha vivido tu vida.
Tu Pana helps you think and revise — but it has not lived your life.

¿Qué puede perder el coach? · What can the coach miss? ▾

The coach can produce writing that sounds smooth, confident, and completely wrong for your assignment. It may miss what you know from your community, your family, or your culture. That is not a failure in you — it is a limitation of the tool.

El coach puede producir texto que suena fluido y seguro — pero completamente equivocado para tu tarea. Puede perder lo que tú sabes desde tu comunidad, tu familia, o tu cultura.

In the next 3 minutes you'll practice the Five Questions — your critical lens for every piece of feedback. This happens once. Las Cinco Preguntas. Toma 3 minutos. Solo una vez.

Why this matters Good writers don't accept AI feedback automatically — they question it first. You're about to practice the five questions that make that judgment a habit.

Read this AI-generated paragraph.

AI-generated revision suggestion Growing up in a bilingual household shaped my cultural identity in significant ways. My family's use of Spanish at home allowed me to maintain a connection to my heritage while also navigating the English-speaking world outside. This experience of living between two languages and two cultures is something that many immigrant families face. Step 2 of 3  ·  Paso 2 de 3

Apply the Five Questions.

Tap your honest assessment of the AI paragraph for each question. You'll see the reasoning after each choice.

① Accuracy
The AI mentions "sociological literature" on assimilation and cultural preservation. Can you trust this claim as written?
The right move: flag it and verify.

The phrase "well-documented in sociological literature" sounds authoritative but cites nothing. AI routinely produces confident-sounding claims without real sources. Before using any historical or academic statement in your own writing, find and read the actual source yourself.

Your coach will never invent citations — but always ask: where does this come from?

② Voice
Does this paragraph still sound like a specific person — with their own memory, language, and way of seeing things?
This paragraph lost its voice.

Phrases like "shaped my cultural identity" could describe anyone. Where is the specific phrase someone in the family said? The word in Spanish that doesn't translate? The exact moment the student felt the difference between the two worlds?

AI smooths writing — and in smoothing it, often erases the details that make it true and powerful. Tu voz importa. Protect it.

③ Specificity
Can you picture a real scene, place, person, or moment from this paragraph?
No scene, no person, no moment.

The paragraph tells us about a bilingual upbringing but never shows it. Strong writing would give us something we can see: a kitchen table, a word a grandmother used, the first day of school in a new language.

Specificity is not decoration — it is the argument. General claims ask readers to believe you. Specific details make them see.

④ Thinking
Does this paragraph help the reader understand something new about the student's experience and its connection to a larger issue?
Description without analysis.

The paragraph mentions "assimilation and cultural preservation" — but it doesn't explain how or why in a way that reveals new understanding.

Strong college writing in any genre asks you to show what your specific material reveals about the larger pattern. That move — from what I have to what it helps explain — is the thinking the work requires.

⑤ Conocimiento · Cultural Knowledge
Does the AI respect what the student knows from their own community, family, or culture? Or does it generalize in ways that erase that knowledge?
The AI generalized what the student knows.

Phrases like "shaped my cultural identity" treat culture as a concept, not as lived knowledge. The student knows specific things: a recipe, a word that has no translation, a ritual, a sound from childhood. AI cannot know these.

When it generalizes, it erases the very knowledge that makes your writing powerful. Tu conocimiento comunitario es evidencia. Do not let the coach replace it with abstractions.
