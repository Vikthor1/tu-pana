# Critical-AI-literacy traceability — Integrated Desk

**Status:** reversible exploration mapping; not a redefinition of Tu Pana doctrine
**Canonical sources inspected:** `assets/js/ui.js` (`EVAL_QUESTIONS`,
`REFLECTION_CHECKPOINTS`), `index.html` (`#fiveQStrip`, Critical Thinking Lab,
Tu Conocimiento), `docs/project-brief.md`, and
`docs/ideas/ai-reflection-checkpoints.md`

Integrated Desk does not invent a new critical-AI framework. It retains the canonical Five
Questions and moves them from a persistent/repeated question surface into authentic mock-AI
decision moments. One relevant question is initially available under **Think critically about this
response**; the remaining framework is optional progressive disclosure. No question blocks return
to writing, and opening a question records only that factual action—not understanding.

## Before / after / decision contract

Before each mock interaction the prototype names the purpose, exact scope and word count, exact-text
preview, mock reviewer or Council roles, represented call count, local-only behavior, student
decision ownership, and explicit consent. Council represents three reviewer calls plus one synthesis;
focused and coach review each represent one call.

After the response, only one contextual canonical question is immediately discoverable. The other
questions remain behind a second disclosure and are not inserted into chat or repeated elsewhere.
The student may ignore the prompt, return to writing, or record Accept, Adapt, Reject, or Decide
later with an optional reason. No decision mutates the Draft.

The ledger stores AI source, payload scope, critical question key and canonical wording, student
decision, optional student-authored rationale, timestamp, Draft signature, and related local version.
Evidence so far may state counts and saved actions. It must not infer that the student noticed bias,
understood the framework, improved judgment, or learned anything.
