# Beyond AI Ghostwriting: Freirean Writing Pedagogy for Multilingual Student Authorship in the Age of Generative AI

*Paulo Freire Conference at Columbia University — September 18–19, 2026*
*Category: Relato de Experiência / Experience Report*
*Submitted by: Víctor M. Torres-Vélez, Hostos Community College, CUNY*

---

## Abstract / Resumo

The proliferation of generative AI writing systems in higher education increasingly threatens to automate the cognitive labor that writing makes possible—displacing student thinking, flattening multilingual voice, and reproducing what Paulo Freire termed the "banking" model of education in algorithmic form. Tu Pana de Escritura is a constrained AI writing coach designed for multilingual, immigrant-background students at Hostos Community College, a Hispanic-Serving Institution (HSI) within the City University of New York. Rather than generating text on students' behalf, the application positions AI strictly as a Socratic interlocutor—structurally prevented from producing essay prose, inventing citations, or replacing student voice. This practice-based report describes the pedagogical architecture underlying Tu Pana: a ten-stage scaffold for a mixed-genre autobiographical essay; a Stage 6 authorship gate requiring an unassisted first draft before any AI-assisted revision; a Voice Vault that protects bilingual language from algorithmic modification; the Five Questions revision framework; and a bilingual parity commitment designed for low-connectivity environments. These design decisions are framed as practical enactments of Freirean anti-banking pedagogy, linguistic justice, and multiliteracies theory, arguing that equitable AI writing tools must constrain AI in order to preserve student epistemic authority.
