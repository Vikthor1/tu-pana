About this pilot

What this pilot is testing

Prueba de pedagogía, accesibilidad, y acompañamiento — not just code.

Tu Pana de Escritura guides students through a 10-stage writing process, from a first personal anecdote to a final reflection on their writing experience. The AI coach responds to student writing — it asks questions, gives feedback, and suggests revision strategies. It does not write for the student.

This pilot is not primarily a technical test. We want to know whether the app is pedagogically sound, linguistically accessible, culturally responsive, and usable in real college writing contexts.

Core design commitment
The student writes first. The AI responds second. The coach should never produce a finished paragraph, introduction, or conclusion for the student. Student voice — including code-switching, Spanglish, and bilingual expression — is treated as a rhetorical resource, not an error to fix.
Setup

Before you begin

Antes de comenzar — a few practical notes for testers.

  • Device: Use a phone, tablet, or computer. Mobile feedback is especially welcome — the app has been recently polished for mobile use.
  • Language: Try Spanish first if you are comfortable. English is fully supported. Switching between languages mid-session is fine.
  • Privacy: Do not enter highly sensitive personal details — immigration status, medical information, financial records, trauma narratives, or any information you would not want stored in a browser session. Use real but safe writing, or a realistic invented sample.
  • Mindset: Notice moments of confusion, friction, or pleasant surprise. Those observations are the most valuable feedback.
  • No right answers: There is no correct way to test this. Use the app the way you think a student would — or the way you think it should be used — and report what you find.

Testing route

Suggested path through the app

You do not have to complete everything. Even partial testing is valuable.

  1. Open the app and notice the first screen. Is the purpose clear?
  2. Notice the language option. Is it easy to find? Does the default feel appropriate?
  3. Complete onboarding. Read through the initial questions and claim your first asset chips.
  4. Write a short anecdote at Stage 1. It can be invented. A paragraph or two is enough.
  5. Ask the coach for help — try at least two messages: one about your writing, one that tests the guardrails (e.g., "Write this paragraph for me" or "Can you write my conclusion?").
  6. Move through Stages 2–3. Notice how the coach shifts focus across stages.
  7. Open Mi Toolkit and explore the three sections: what you bring, skills practiced, and Beyond This Essay.
  8. Attempt Stage 6 to see the first-draft authorship gate.
  9. If time allows, try Stage 7 (Revision) or Stage 8 (Voice Polish).
  10. If possible, visit Stage 10 to see the final process reflection area and the instructor report.
  11. Send your feedback using the questions in this packet.
Short test — 15–20 min Onboarding + Stages 1–3 + Mi Toolkit + feedback questions. Ideal if time is limited.
Full test — 45–60 min Complete path through Stage 10, including revision and voice polish. Ideal for deep pedagogical review.
Observation guide

What to look for

Use these as a mental checklist while testing — or mark them after.

Clarity
  • I understood what to do at each stage.
  • The purpose of the coach was clear.
  • Stage transitions felt logical.
  • Onboarding explained the app's purpose without being overwhelming.
Trust and Safety
  • I felt safe entering my writing into the app.
  • The app did not pressure me to share sensitive information.
  • The coach behaved in a way I would trust with students.
Writing Pedagogy
  • The coach helped me think about my writing rather than doing it for me.
  • The authorship gate at Stage 6 made its purpose clear.
  • The Five Questions revision protocol (Stage 7) felt useful.
  • Stage 8 (Voice Polish) protected my voice rather than erasing it.
Voice and Language
  • The bilingual design felt natural, not forced.
  • Code-switching or Spanglish was not treated as an error.
  • The coach responded appropriately in the language I used.
  • Spanish felt equally valued, not like a translation of English.
Mobile Usability
  • The app was usable on my device.
  • Buttons and interactive elements were easy to tap.
  • Mi Toolkit opened and closed cleanly on mobile.
  • Text was readable without zooming.
Mi Toolkit
  • Mi Toolkit opened without problems.
  • I understood the three sections: what I bring, skills practiced, and Beyond This Essay.
  • The skills I practiced were visible after completing stages.
  • The "Beyond This Essay" cards felt relevant to learning outside the app.
AI Coach Behavior
  • The coach stayed in its role — feedback and questions, not essay writing.
  • When I asked it to write for me, it appropriately declined or redirected.
  • The coach never invented sources or facts.
  • The coach did not feel generic — it responded to what I actually wrote.

Privacy

Safe testing reminder

Recordatorio de privacidad — for all testers.

Important — Please read before entering any personal writing
  • Do not enter immigration status, legal records, medical information, financial details, or any information you would not share publicly.
  • Do not paste confidential student work without the student's permission.
  • Use realistic but invented or lightly fictionalized writing whenever possible.
  • This pilot tests the writing support experience — it does not require you to disclose private information to generate useful feedback.
  • Your browser session data (localStorage) is stored locally on your device and is cleared when you reset the app. Nothing is sent to a central server except the AI coach messages processed through the app's secure proxy.
Sample writing prompt

If you are not sure what to write

Use this prompt to get started — or invent something of your own.

English

"Write about a moment when a place, language, family memory, migration story, neighborhood experience, or classroom experience shaped how you understand yourself or the world."

Español

"Escribe sobre un momento en que un lugar, una lengua, una memoria familiar, una historia de migración, una experiencia del barrio o una experiencia escolar cambió la manera en que entiendes quién eres o el mundo que te rodea."

Tester note
You do not need a real memory to test the app. A short invented scenario — three to five sentences — is enough to move through the early stages and trigger meaningful coach responses.
