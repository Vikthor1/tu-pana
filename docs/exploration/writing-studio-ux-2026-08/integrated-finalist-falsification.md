# Integrated Desk finalist — falsification guide

**Classification:** founder review instrument; no architecture selected

The finalist hypothesis is: **Desk can add durable novice guidance, contextual critical-AI
judgment, and assignment-relevant cultural or disciplinary knowledge without adding another mental
model or restoring interface accumulation.** A successful automated path does not confirm it.

## Principal comparison with plain Desk

- Is the Draft still the unmistakable home of the student's real writing?
- Can a first-time student say, without instruction, “My essay lives in the Draft. Moves and notes
  help me think”?
- Does added guidance remain calm compared with plain Desk?
- Are Move notes valuable, discoverable, and clearly reference-only?
- Does **Ask Tu Pana** correct the prior ambiguity, or is coach access still mistaken for a status
  strip or hidden review feature?
- Does the cultural first-use moment feel relevant, optional, and revisitable rather than imposed?
- Does one contextual canonical Five Question deepen judgment without creating fatigue or homework?
- Is the integrated interface still understandable on a phone?

## Architecture and pedagogy failure signals

- Students treat Moves as a mandatory sequence or ask which Move must be completed next.
- Navigation alone appears to complete a Move, or an empty note is counted as progress.
- Students write finished essay prose in Move notes or expect notes to assemble the Draft.
- Planning notes become “Notebook & Draft” under another name or feel like a competing work home.
- Students who draft directly feel warned, behind, incomplete, or locked out.
- Added panels make Integrated Desk harder to predict than Hybrid's four phases.
- Students cannot answer “Where is my real work?” more confidently than in Journey.
- The finalist preserves less useful novice scaffolding than Notebook or makes that scaffolding harder
  to retrieve.

## Cultural and translingual failure signals

- Autobiographical onboarding implies that cultural identity, family, community, trauma, or multilingual
  performance is expected.
- Students cannot skip the lens without feeling penalized.
- Review genericizes, flattens, stereotypes, depoliticizes, or misreads culturally situated writing,
  or treats code-meshing as an error.
- STEM, SOP, admissions, or General Writing shows mixed-genre autobiographical Moves or onboarding.
- General Writing becomes a silent autobiographical fallback, or an unknown assignment inherits
  either profile instead of requiring selection.
- An unavailable disciplinary Council silently falls back to unrelated roles.

## Critical-AI-literacy and trust failure signals

- Students consent without identifying the exact payload, purpose, reviewer, or represented calls.
- The Five Questions appear as a repeated wall, blocking questionnaire, or coach-chat interruption.
- Students believe Accept or Adapt changed the Draft automatically.
- The optional rationale is experienced as required or is mistaken for AI-generated prose.
- Evidence so far claims understanding, bias detection, voice preservation, or learning not
  demonstrated by student-authored evidence.
- Save, Finish, Create packet, Backup, or external Submit are conflated.

## Mobile and accessibility failure signals

- Ask Tu Pana or Planning notes obscure the Draft or lose orientation at 390 × 844 or on a physical
  phone.
- Passage Tray moves offscreen with the real iOS keyboard, loses exact selection, or conflicts with
  native selection handles.
- Focus hides Exit or the passage action.
- 200–400% zoom, language choice, VoiceOver, or safe-area behavior creates duplicated, clipped, or
  horizontally scrolling controls.

## Evidence still needed

Run the full founder journey in plain Desk and Integrated Desk using the same synthetic assignment,
then repeat the relevant path in Notebook, Hybrid, and Journey. Record prediction, hesitation,
error, recovery, and unprompted explanations—not preference alone. Repeat with representative
students, a physical iPhone with the keyboard open, VoiceOver, English-only, Spanish-only, and
optional bilingual modes. Do not promote the finalist from reachability or founder preference.
