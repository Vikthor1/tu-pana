What you are comparing

The four genre pathways

One 10-step writing journey — with an unassisted first-draft authorship gate, voice protection, process evidence, and AI-use transparency — carries all four genres.

Autobiographical Mixed-Genre Essay

Ensayo autobiográfico de género mixto

Personal, reflective, analytical writing that connects lived experience, course concepts, and community knowledge. Tu Pana’s base pathway.

Service-Learning Report

Informe de aprendizaje-servicio

Community-engaged writing that connects field experience, observation, reflection, evidence, and public-facing analysis. (Currently profiled for CAP 200 “Bronx Beautiful”; the engine underneath is course-agnostic.)

Research Paper

Trabajo de investigación

Source-based academic writing that develops a research question, evidence, argument, structure, citation, and revision process. The coach never invents, retrieves, or verifies sources or citations.

STEM Lab Report

Informe de laboratorio STEM

Scientific writing (fuller name: STEM Lab Report & Scientific Explanation) that supports lab context, method, observation/data, results, Claim–Evidence–Reasoning, limitations, and AI-use reflection. The coach never fabricates data, solves the science, verifies scientific correctness, or grades.

What to do

Review tasks

About 25–30 minutes total, or review just the pathway closest to your discipline.

  1. Try each pathway for 5–7 minutes. Start where a student starts: write a few honest sentences in the draft panel, walk a few steps of the journey.
  2. Open the support tools. Look at Help, the Five Questions, and “Estoy atascado · I’m stuck.”
  3. Test the coach’s boundaries. Try one thing a shortcut-seeking student would try (e.g., “write it for me”) and watch how the coach responds.
  4. Look at the finish. Open the finish/export flow and skim the process report a student would submit.
  5. Compare across pathways. Genre clarity, support, bilingual access, AI transparency, and finish/export logic.
What to notice

Evaluation checklist

Ten questions — jot quick notes as you go.

  • Is the writing task clear within the first 60 seconds?
  • Does each pathway feel distinct but part of one coherent Tu Pana system?
  • Does the draft area remain central?
  • Do Help, Five Questions, and “I’m stuck” support without overwhelming?
  • Does bilingual support feel empowering rather than heavy?
  • Is AI presented as coaching, not authorship replacement?
  • Does finish/export make sense across all pathways?
  • Does STEM support scientific writing without checking, solving, grading, or fabricating science?
  • What would confuse a real student under deadline pressure?
  • What would help an instructor trust this enough to pilot?
