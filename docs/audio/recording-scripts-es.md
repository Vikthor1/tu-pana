## Context

Browser-native TTS was evaluated and abandoned (dialect mismatch — es-ES vs Caribbean/Latinx Spanish). These scripts replace synthetic speech with human-recorded audio assets. Target directory: `assets/audio/es/`.

The scripts are designed to **complement** the screen, not read it verbatim. The voice should feel like a supportive professor accompanying the student through the experience.

## Part 2 — Scripts

---

### FILE: `landing-welcome-es.mp3`

**Purpose:** First-time landing overlay. Student sees the opening quote and privacy note. This is the first human voice they hear.

**Estimated duration:** 25–30 seconds

**Voice direction:** The slowest, most grounded recording of the set. This is not a tutorial — it is a welcome. Settle before you start. Say "Bienvenido, bienvenida" as if a student just walked through your office door. Natural pause between the second and third sentence. Do not rush the final line.

**Script:**

> Bienvenido, bienvenida. Soy el profesor Torres-Vélez.
>
> Antes de conocer a tu coach de escritura, quiero que sepas algo: la historia que tú traes — tu idioma, tu comunidad, tu trayectoria — eso no es un obstáculo. Es exactamente donde empieza tu escritura.
>
> Cuando estés listo o lista, continúa.

---

### FILE: `mani-intro-es.mp3`

**Purpose:** Tu Conocimiento opening card. Student sees the heading "Tú ya produces conocimiento" and five unclaimed asset cards. Plays before any interaction.

**Estimated duration:** 30–35 seconds

**Voice direction:** Conviction without performance. The screen already makes the claim — your voice confirms it as something you genuinely believe, not something you're announcing. Slight emphasis on "ya" in the first sentence. Natural weight on "desde adentro." The final two sentences should feel like an invitation, not an instruction.

**Script:**

> Esta pantalla tiene una sola idea, y es una idea real: tú ya llegaste aquí con conocimiento.
>
> Lo que sabes desde tu familia, desde tu barrio, desde tus idiomas — eso es exactamente lo que la universidad necesita que lleves a tu escritura. No como decoración. Como argumento.
>
> Toca cada uno. Reclámalos.

---

### FILE: `mani-freire-es.mp3`

**Purpose:** Appears after all five assets are claimed, when the Freire writing prompt becomes visible. Student is invited to write one sentence.

**Estimated duration:** 18–22 seconds

**Voice direction:** Quieter than the intro. This is an invitation to reflect, not a task to complete. No urgency. Slight pause after "una sola oración." The word "honesto" should land softly.

**Script:**

> Ahora, una sola oración.
>
> Escribe algo que tú sabes desde tu propia experiencia — algo que tu profesor o profesora quizás no sabe. No tiene que ser largo. No tiene que ser perfecto. Solo tiene que ser honesto.

---

### FILE: `lab-step0-es.mp3`

**Purpose:** El Laboratorio, Step 0. Student sees: "Your coach is powerful. Your judgment is more powerful." This is the most pedagogically critical recording in the set.

**Estimated duration:** 38–44 seconds

**Voice direction:** Clear and unhurried. This is not a disclaimer — it is the central intellectual claim of the whole application. "Tiene límites reales" should land with calm honesty, not alarm. A beat of silence after that phrase before continuing is effective. "Tu juicio siempre tiene la última palabra" is the most important sentence — say it as if you mean it.

**Script:**

> Antes de conocer al coach, vamos a hablar de algo importante.
>
> Tu Pana es una herramienta poderosa — puede ayudarte a pensar, a organizar, a revisar. Pero tiene límites reales. No ha vivido tu historia. No conoce tu comunidad. No puede saber lo que tú sabes desde adentro.
>
> En los próximos minutos vas a practicar las Cinco Preguntas — el lente crítico que vas a usar cada vez que el coach te responda.
>
> Tu juicio siempre tiene la última palabra.

---

### FILE: `lab-step1-es.mp3`

**Purpose:** Step 1. Student is about to read an AI-generated paragraph before evaluating it.

**Estimated duration:** 15–18 seconds

**Voice direction:** Neutral and investigative — like assigning a close reading. Do not hint at whether the paragraph is good or bad. Let the student discover it. Slight pause before "Léelo con cuidado."

**Script:**

> Aquí hay un párrafo que generó el coach cuando un estudiante pidió ayuda con su ensayo.
>
> Léelo con cuidado. Tómate el tiempo que necesites. Después lo analizamos juntos.

---

### FILE: `lab-step2-es.mp3`

**Purpose:** Step 2 intro — before the Five Questions quiz begins.

**Estimated duration:** 18–22 seconds

**Voice direction:** Inviting and calm. This is practice, not a test — the student should feel that. Emphasize "honesta" gently. The word "verás" at the end should carry a slight sense of discovery.

**Script:**

> Ahora vamos a aplicar las cinco preguntas a ese párrafo.
>
> No hay respuestas incorrectas — esto es práctica. Lo que importa es tu evaluación honesta. Selecciona lo que piensas para cada pregunta, y verás el análisis.

---

### FILE: `lab-step3-es.mp3`

**Purpose:** Step 3 — summary and launch. Student has completed the Five Questions exercise and is about to enter the app.

**Estimated duration:** 28–34 seconds

**Voice direction:** Confident but not triumphant. The student has done real work — acknowledge it simply. "Tu historia, tu voz" should feel specific to this student, not generic. Take a full breath before "Ahora sí, empecemos." That pause is the door opening.

**Script:**

> Ya tienes las cinco preguntas. Las vas a usar cada vez que el coach te dé una respuesta — no para bloquear su ayuda, sino para evaluarla con criterio.
>
> Tu historia y tu voz son lo que hace que tu ensayo sea tuyo. El coach te puede acompañar, pero no puede reemplazarte.
>
> *(pausa)*
>
> Ahora sí, empecemos.

*Note: `*(pausa)*` is a breath cue — do not speak it. Allow ~1.5 seconds of silence.*

## General Recording Notes

- Record each file as a standalone take. Each script is self-contained.
- Aim for low ambient noise. A small carpeted office is sufficient.
- Leave 1–2 seconds of silence at the start and end of each recording for clean trimming.
- The `*(pausa)*` in `lab-step3-es.mp3` is a breath cue (~1.5 seconds), not a gap.
- English versions are deferred by design. Produce after Spanish versions are reviewed with students and audio playback integration is built.
